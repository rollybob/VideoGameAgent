# Font sources

- pokemon-ds.ttf  : DS-style fan font (F1/F2 stopgap; NOT the FRLG in-game font).
- pokemon-firered.ttf : "Pokemon Fire Red" by KaelinT (CC-BY-SA, commercial OK).
  Source: https://font.download/font/pokemon-fire-red . Installed 2026-06-28 (F3) as a
  close recreation of the FRLG dialogue/menu font after the DS font lost to Tesseract
  on real frames (CER 0.31 vs 0.042). Visually validated glyph-shape match vs real
  captured frames before retraining. Final arbiter = data/bench_pokemon benchmark.
