You are on the world map and must travel to Giza Plains. Follow this exact procedure:
1. Press Start ONE time to open the menu.
2. The menu lists Party, Area List, System. Press Down to highlight "Area List", then press A.
3. The Area List shows Cyril, Sprohm, Giza Plains. Press Down until "Giza Plains" is highlighted, then press A.
4. Press A once more to confirm, then your party begins walking there.
IMPORTANT: Once any menu is open, do NOT press Start again - Start closes the menu. Inside menus use ONLY the d-pad and the A button. After confirming, choose "wait" while your party walks to Giza.
