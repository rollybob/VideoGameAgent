You are in a town in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance. Your task, in order: (1) At the pub, open the Missions list, select the "Herb Picking" mission and accept it (choose Yes to accept the conditions). (2) Leave the pub and exit the town to the world map (press B to back out of menus; choose any exit/leave option you see). (3) On the world map, travel to Giza Plains, the mission site, to start the mission battle. Navigate with the d-pad, confirm with A, cancel/back out with B.
