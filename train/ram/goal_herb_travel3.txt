You are at the pub. Open the Missions list, select the "Herb Picking" mission, and accept it (choose Yes when asked to accept the conditions). Once the mission is accepted, leave the pub and travel to Giza Plains on the world map to begin the mission. To travel on the world map: press Start, choose "Area List", then choose "Giza Plains" from the list. Navigate menus with the d-pad and confirm with A.
